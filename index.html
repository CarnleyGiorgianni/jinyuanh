// About the Studio

Built on Three Pillars of Innovation

Minimalism in code. Privacy in architecture. Zen in experience. We translate complex technology into calm, beautiful, secure products.

Minimalist Engineering

We obsess over removing what is not necessary. Each line of code, every pixel, every interaction earns its place. The result is software that feels effortless, fast, and focused.

Your data belongs to you. Our products operate fully on-device — no surveillance, no cloud tracking, no compromise. Privacy is not a feature; it is the foundation of trust.

Immersive UX

// Frequently Asked

Common Questions

Do you collect any of my data?

No. By design, your recordings, routes, collection entries, and goals stay on your device.

Which App Store regions are supported?

All major regions: US, UK, EU, Canada, Australia, Japan, Korea, Singapore, Hong Kong, Taiwan, India, Brazil, Mexico.

Do your apps work offline?

Yes — every Jinyuanh app is engineered to function fully without internet. Online connectivity is only for opt-in features.

How can I delete my data?

Use the in-app deletion feature, or simply uninstall the app. For accounts, email contact@jinyuanh.com for full deletion.

How is the studio funded?

Premium app tiers, optional in-app advertising in free tiers, and bespoke engineering partnerships.

Do you accept work-for-hire projects?

Yes. We partner with select studios and brands. Reach out via contact@jinyuanh.com with your project brief.
